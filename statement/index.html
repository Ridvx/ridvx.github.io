ARTIST STATEMENT

RIDVX VISUAL

I am a new media artist, visual jockey (VJ), and motion graphics designer based in Tulungagung, East Java. My artistic practice moves between motion graphics, projection mapping, and new media installations. Mediums that I use not merely as presentation tools, but as a language to translate local issues into audiovisual experiences that can be directly felt by the body and senses. Tulungagung, the place where I live and work, serves as my personal laboratory to explore the intersection between animism and algorithms. Technology, in my practice, does not come to replace local ancestral values and spirituality, but rather serves as a new vessel to preserve them. That intersection is the core of my concept.

I translate this idea through a working method that believes research-based art does not need to be oversimplified to be understood. Complexity can instead be presented wholly through media art.By breaking down a grand concept into fragments of mediums (light, sound, motion, objects) that are reassembled within a single installation. The audience does not receive a ready-made conclusion, but is invited to piece together those fragments into meaning themselves. For me, that is how research transforms into an experience, rather than mere information.

It is this same method of breaking down and reassembling that I use to trace the relationship between technology and science and their impact on humanity.Questions regarding how the tools we create silently alter the way we think, believe, and connect with one another. Likewise, my works strive to present these questions not as a frightening warning, but as a shared space for reflection: to what extent do we still hold control over the technology we create, and to what extent does it shape us in return.
